❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Will my text be saved online?

No, your text is only stored locally in your browser for convenience. It is not uploaded anywhere.

How are words counted?

Words are counted by separating the text based on spaces. Multiple spaces or new lines are ignored.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! The tool is fully responsive and works on any device.

Can I clear my data?

Yes, use the ❌ Clear button to reset the text and counts immediately.
